==== SECTION 5  DSI pages and the 0x800-vs-0x1000 stride (Q1/Q2) ====
If DSI1 sits at 0x6B800 then 0x6C000 is DPCLKA, not a timing block. The
two readings are separable ONLY if something programmed a DSI transcoder,
and nothing on an eDP-only laptop ever will -- so a null here settles
nothing either way. Recorded so the absence is documented rather than
inferred.
  0x6B000 DSI0 HTOTAL      0x00000000
  0x6B800 DSI1 HTOTAL      0x00000000
  0x6C000 DPCLKA-or-DSI1   0x00000000
  0x7B000 pipe DSI0        0x00000000
  0x7B800 pipe DSI1        0x00000000
